About Hibiscus Design Limited

Local, established, and here to help

Our Story

Hibiscus Design Limited has been serving the Lingfield community since 2016. We're based at Bluebell Cottage, right in the heart of the area we work in, which means we understand the local gardens and the local climate.

We see ourselves as your local gardening experts, dedicated to transforming outdoor spaces into something special — whatever that means for your particular garden.

We're open Monday to Saturday, 8am–6pm, ready to help bring your garden vision to life.
